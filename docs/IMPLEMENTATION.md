# Implementation boundaries

The latest implementation brief was prioritized over an earlier alternative concept. Private planning pages are not copied into this repository.

## Implemented

- A restrained, museum-like documentary instead of a game or a cluttered mission dashboard.
- Four-chapter cosmic/context opening using NASA catalogue imagery, a NASA solar-system illustration, Apollo Earthrise and a NASA mission-control preview.
- Ten spotlight stories: five Moon, five Mars. Automatic readable timing; pause/resume, manual next, skip and exit; Moon/Mars switching; final legacy chapter.
- Eighteen full records including the broader inventory. Beyond-Moon/Mars missions live separately and are never plotted on the lunar/Martian surface.
- Interactive NASA-textured globes and selectable, source-qualified regional/landing-site markers. Full text selection lists. Keyboard and touch support.
- Two original NASA STL terrain references, inspectable in 3D. These are **not** georeferenced navigation scenes, full-world terrain, or reconstructed spacecraft placement.
- NASA 3D reference models and published images, with model caveats and original-source credits. Original NASA models are not altered to fake their final appearance.
- Purpose, scientific results, why hardware remained, nuanced end status, location uncertainty and claim-specific source explanations.
- Search/filter, deep links, local visited markers, source archive, downloadable original assets and per-file hashes.
- Desktop/tablet/mobile layouts, readable transcripts, reduced-motion support and a WebGL image/text fallback.
- A Moon/Mars synthesis, forward-looking design lessons and an explanatory reflection question.
- Self-hosted NASA assets, fonts and Draco decoders. No API credentials or server costs are required by the app.

## Deliberately not fabricated

- **No generated fly-through, particle field, animated astronaut or recreated spacecraft footage.** User-directed 3D rotation/zoom is functional inspection, not a generated cinematic animation. NASA images and original models do the visual work.
- **No ElevenLabs recording or mascot impersonation.** The brief did not supply a final astronaut/mascot reference, voice choice or audio credentials. The working fallback is opt-in browser/device read-aloud with identical visible text. This is not advertised as ElevenLabs or a real astronaut voice. A future recorded narration pass can use the existing text, with explicit voice rights and a properly licensed audio source.
- **No live NASA health feed.** All statuses are dated, source-based historical records. The 2019 reflector article is not evidence of current health in 2026. Ingenuity's flight-mission ending is not described as universal electronic failure.
- **No made-up price tags.** Individual hardware costs are omitted rather than substituting a whole-mission budget.
- **No fabricated science or emotional quotations.** Water/habitability findings are not evidence of life. No “last words” are invented for Opportunity.
- **No precise final coordinates where sources only establish a region.** Opportunity's approximate final-region pin differs from its original Eagle crater landing site. InSight/Ingenuity/Sojourner/Spirit markers are explicitly regional.
- **No official sponsorship, submitted entry, tested-on-children claim or promised judging outcome.** Submission, team registration, eligibility and any human validation remain the team's responsibility.

## Remaining polish opportunities

A properly licensed, approved recorded narration track; verified final-position coordinates from dedicated cartographic products; additional original NASA media captions/individual contributor audits; screen-reader checks with real assistive technology; and testing on physical mobile hardware. These are not claimed complete by automated tests.
