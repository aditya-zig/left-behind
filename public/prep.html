What did we build?

Left Behind is an interactive space museum. It explains what NASA left on the Moon and Mars, why it stayed, and what we learned.

Think of a museum exhibit: you see a machine, learn its story, and can check where the information came from.

This is your self-check, not a readiness score.

You are explaining a science-communication project. You do not have to know all of NASA.
